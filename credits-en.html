Data and libraries

Maps use © OpenStreetMap contributors data under ODbL and the OpenMapTiles schema with its attribution requirements. OpenFreeMap delivers maps and MapLibre Native (BSD-2-Clause) renders them. Photon provides search; OSRM / FOSSGIS provides online routing. The interface uses AndroidX / Jetpack Compose.

The links below identify licences and providers. This does not mean their entire code is included in Vektor or that they endorse the app. Guidance speech is generated by the selected system TTS engine, not a recorded developer voice.

Licence notices

Third-party materials remain owned by their respective authors. A store release requires a complete direct/transitive dependency review and preservation of all required licence notices with the build. Do not remove map attribution when editing the interface.
