Privacy Policy

Vektor · 30.10.2026 · v1.0

1. Who is responsible

Developer and contact: Krupnik Hlieb / Крупнік Глєб, Ukraine. Privacy and support contact: support.vektor@gmail.com. This policy covers the Vektor Android test application and its download website. It describes the current build, not future features.

2. Data used by the application

Location data includes coordinates, reported accuracy, update time and provider, and speed and travel bearing when available. Accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer and step readings support phone direction and approximate motion estimation when a fresh position is unavailable. These readings are processed on the phone; this does not involve audio or video recording.

The app also uses address/place queries, origin and destination points, travel mode, saved and recent places, local road reports and settings. To select speech output, it checks installed text-to-speech services and their voices, not a general inventory of all installed applications.

3. Purposes and permissions

Data supports place search, route planning and display, turn guidance, map orientation, speech and reopening an unfinished trip. Location access uses Android permissions. An active trip can continue with the screen locked through a foreground service with an ongoing notification; End stops the trip. Physical activity access supports step detection, and notification permission supports the navigation card. Camera, microphone, contacts and SMS permissions are not requested for these features.

You may refuse or revoke permissions in Android, limiting the relevant functions. Processing serves functions you request and uses consent/permissions where legally required. A system permission is not unlimited consent for unrelated purposes.

4. Requests to external services

OpenFreeMap receives style, font and tile requests for the map area being viewed. These requests disclose that area.

Photon (photon.komoot.io) receives search text, including suggestions after a pause in typing, and may receive point A/current coordinates to favour nearby results. A reverse lookup to identify a city for a short address may also transmit coordinates.

OSRM / FOSSGIS (routing.openstreetmap.de) receives route origin, destination and travel mode. Automatic rerouting may use the current confirmed position as the origin.

Each network connection exposes the IP address and request metadata to its server. App requests to these APIs use HTTPS. Providers may process data outside Ukraine under their own policies; Vektor’s developer does not control their retention periods.

Android and its location provider separately govern satellite and network positioning. The selected speech engine receives guidance text; a network voice may send that text to its provider. Offline speech requires a suitable downloaded voice.

5. Storage on the phone

Vektor’s private app data stores settings, up to 40 saved and 15 recent places, the last accepted/manually confirmed position anchor, up to 100 local road reports and one unfinished route. Search caching is bounded in process memory; MapLibre manages map caching.

An unfinished route is removed on End or confirmed arrival; a record older than seven days is not offered and is deleted when read. Report lifetimes range from 30 minutes to seven days by category; expired entries are pruned when loaded or while reports are maintained. Saved places, settings and the anchor remain until changed or app data is cleared.

This update disables standard Android app backup and data transfer through package configuration. Manufacturer-specific behaviour and independent backups you create remain outside the developer’s control.

This setting does not delete backups created previously; managing them depends on Android and the backup provider.

6. Features not present

The developer has not added account registration, advertising, advertising identifiers, an analytics SDK or a server storing your trips in this build. Road reports are local and are not published to other drivers. Raw sensor readings are not sent to the developer. This does not mean that external map services, Android, speech engines or hosting providers receive no requests: the requests described above still apply.

7. Website and APK downloads

The website does not request browser location and has no developer-operated sign-up forms, advertising or analytics. Browser localStorage stores the language preference under vektor-language; clear site data to remove it. The website sets no first-party advertising or analytics cookies.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes technical visit information including IP addresses. Downloads through GitHub Releases also contact GitHub and its infrastructure. Following an external link opens another service governed by its own policy. Saving a language preference is not consent to advertising tracking.

8. Requests, rights and deletion

You may request information about processing, correction or deletion, withdraw consent where it is the basis, and contact the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights. Contact: support.vektor@gmail.com. Do not send a precise home route or other unnecessary sensitive details for a routine support question.

The developer cannot remotely access data stored on your phone. Saved places and reports can be deleted in the app; remove all local data through Android → apps → Vektor → storage → clear data, or uninstall. End an active trip first. Local deletion does not erase independent provider logs; their policies apply.

If you voluntarily send support email or diagnostics, the developer receives the sender address, content and attachments to respond. Correspondence is kept only as reasonably necessary to resolve the issue and meet legal obligations; deletion may be requested through the same channel.

9. Security, children and updates

The app uses Android private storage and HTTPS for the listed APIs; absolute security cannot be guaranteed. This test version is not directed specifically at children and does not ask for a birth date. Involvement of a legal representative must be arranged when legally required.

Material changes to purposes, recipients or data categories will be disclosed through an updated policy and, where needed, an in-app notice before new processing. Shared reports or analytics would require a separate policy review. The document version and date appear above.
